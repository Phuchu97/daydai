# SEO Execution Playbook (Vietnam)

Muc tieu: day nhom tu khoa "day dai thep / day dai sat / khoa dai thep / bo dung cu dong dai thep" len top cao tai Google VN.

## 1) Search Console setup checklist (phai lam ngay)

- Xac minh domain trong Google Search Console:
  - Da ho tro trong code: bien `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` (metadata verification).
  - Sau khi xac minh, gui sitemap: `https://daydaivn.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Bat dau thu thap du lieu:
  - Performance > Search results > country = Vietnam.
  - Device split: mobile/desktop.
  - Query filter cho nhom:
    - `day dai thep`
    - `day dai sat`
    - `khoa dai thep`
    - `bo dung cu dong dai thep`
    - `day dai thep ha noi`
    - `day dai thep mien bac`
- Kiem tra index:
  - URL Inspection cho cac trang tien:
    - `/bao-gia-day-dai-thep`
    - `/day-dai-thep-16mm-19mm-32mm`
    - `/khoa-dai-thep`
    - `/bo-dung-cu-dong-dai-thep`
    - `/day-dai-thep-ha-noi`
    - `/day-dai-thep-mien-bac`
    - `/day-dai-sat`
    - `/dai-thep-cong-nghiep`
    - `/nha-cung-cap-day-dai-thep`

## 2) Query-to-page mapping (tranh canibalization)

### Core commercial
- `day dai thep`, `dai thep`: `/san-pham`, `/day-dai-thep-16mm-19mm-32mm`
- `bao gia day dai thep`, `gia day dai thep`: `/bao-gia-day-dai-thep`
- `khoa dai thep`, `bo sat dong dai`: `/khoa-dai-thep`
- `bo dung cu dong dai thep`: `/bo-dung-cu-dong-dai-thep`

### Local commercial
- `day dai thep ha noi`: `/day-dai-thep-ha-noi`
- `day dai thep mien bac`, `nha cung cap day dai thep mien bac`: `/day-dai-thep-mien-bac`

### Synonym / long-tail
- `day dai sat`, `dai sat dong hang`: `/day-dai-sat`
- `dai thep cong nghiep`: `/dai-thep-cong-nghiep`
- `nha cung cap day dai thep`: `/nha-cung-cap-day-dai-thep`

## 3) Internal anchor map (de day authority)

Muc tieu: moi bai `tu-van` phai co 3-5 anchor tro ve money pages.

- Anchor chinh:
  - "bao gia day dai thep" -> `/bao-gia-day-dai-thep`
  - "day dai thep 16mm 19mm 32mm" -> `/day-dai-thep-16mm-19mm-32mm`
  - "khoa dai thep" -> `/khoa-dai-thep`
  - "bo dung cu dong dai thep" -> `/bo-dung-cu-dong-dai-thep`
  - "day dai thep ha noi" -> `/day-dai-thep-ha-noi`
  - "day dai thep mien bac" -> `/day-dai-thep-mien-bac`

Nguyen tac:
- Anchor dung nghia, khong nhoi tu khoa.
- 1 URL nhan 2-3 anchor bien the (tranh trung lap may moc).
- Moi bai moi phai link toi it nhat 2 money pages.

## 4) On-page KPI va nguong canh bao

Theo doi hang tuan (7 ngay):
- Impressions tang >= 15% / thang cho nhom query muc tieu.
- CTR:
  - Query vi tri 1-5: muc tieu CTR >= 8-12%
  - Query vi tri 6-10: muc tieu CTR >= 4-8%
- Vi tri trung binh:
  - Query chinh: day vao top 20 trong 45-60 ngay
  - Long-tail: top 10 trong 30-45 ngay

Canh bao:
- Impressions tang ma CTR giam -> sua title/meta ngay.
- Vi tri dung yen > 3 tuan -> tang backlink + cap nhat noi dung.
- URL co impressions nhung click thap -> bo sung FAQ/USP ngay dau trang.

## 5) Content operations (30-60 ngay)

Lich dang bai:
- 2 bai/tuan trong 8 tuan (16 bai).
- Ty le:
  - 50% transactional/commercial
  - 30% how-to ky thuat
  - 20% so sanh/case thuc te

Chu de uu tien:
- Bao gia day dai thep theo quy cach 16/19/32mm
- Cach chon khoa dai cho tung ban day
- Loi tuot moi dai va cach khac phuc
- Day dai thep cho pallet thep, cuon thep, vat lieu xay dung
- Day dai thep ha noi / mien bac (intent local)

## 6) Backlink outreach plan (off-page)

Nguon uu tien:
- Website nganh cong nghiep, co khi, logistics, xay dung.
- Danh ba doanh nghiep B2B co uy tin.
- Bao/PR nganh vat tu dong goi.

Muc tieu 60 ngay:
- 20-30 referring domains moi (uu tien chat luong hon so luong).
- 5-8 backlink contextual ve 3 money pages chinh.

### Outreach template (VN)

Subject: Hop tac noi dung ve day dai thep cho doanh nghiep

Xin chao [Ten don vi],

Ben em dang xay dung chuoi noi dung huong dan thuc te ve day dai thep, khoa dai va dung cu dong dai cho kho/nhamay. Ben anh/chị co the hop tac dang bai chuyen mon hoac case study lien quan khong?

Ben em co san:
- Noi dung ky thuat goc, khong sao chep
- Hinh anh thuc te quy trinh dong dai
- Data checklist cho doanh nghiep B2B

Neu phu hop, ben em gui outline de doi ngu minh duyet nhanh.

Cam on anh/chị.

## 7) Chu ky toi uu hang tuan (operational loop)

Moi thu 2:
- Doc Search Console 7 ngay gan nhat.
- Chot 5 query can toi uu title/meta.

Moi thu 4:
- Cap nhat 2 trang co CTR thap.
- Them 3 internal links moi tu bai tu van ve money pages.

Moi thu 6:
- Dang 1 bai moi + 1 cap nhat bai cu.
- Gui 5 outreach emails backlink.
